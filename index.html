Methodology & Standard Principles

Statistical Principles & Analytical Guidelines

All calibrations adhere strictly to international metrological frameworks (JCGM 100:2008 GUM) and geochronological standards (Ludwig Isoplot).

1. Weighted Regression (WLS)

Multi-Point Fit

Measurements with higher precision (smaller 1σ SD) are assigned greater statistical weight ($w_i = 1/\sigma_i^2$) across 2 or more anchor standards.

* A run with 2x higher uncertainty exerts only 1/4 the statistical leverage on the calibration slope.
Basis: York (1968) / Ludwig (2003)

2. Goodness-of-Fit (MSWD)

Fit Quality

Evaluates the reduced chi-squared ($\chi^2/\nu$) to objectively determine whether residuals reflect pure analytical precision.

* An MSWD close to 1 indicates an ideal fit; values >> 1 indicate unmodeled instrumental drift or non-analytical variance.
Basis: McIntyre et al. (1966) / IsoplotR

3. Combined Uncertainty

Total Uncertainty

Propagates sample repeatability together with the standard errors of the regression parameters.

* Provides peer-review-proof uncertainty budgets that satisfy editorial requirements of major journals.
Basis: JCGM 100:2008 (GUM Guide)

A. Weighted Least Squares Normal Equations

Exact analytical solution minimizing the sum of weighted squared residuals $\sum w_i (y_i - ax_i - b)^2$:

$$w_i = \frac{1}{\sigma_{x,i}^2}, \quad \Delta = \sum w_i \sum w_i x_i^2 - \left(\sum w_i x_i\right)^2$$ $$\text{Slope }(a) = \frac{\sum w_i \sum w_i x_i y_i - \sum w_i x_i \sum w_i y_i}{\Delta}, \quad \text{Intercept }(b) = \frac{\sum w_i x_i^2 \sum w_i y_i - \sum w_i x_i \sum w_i x_i y_i}{\Delta}$$
B. EA-IRMS Blank Mass Balance & Size Linearity Correction

Mass-balance blank subtraction and peak-area linearity normalization prior to WLS scale calibration:

$$\delta_{\text{blk-corr}} = \frac{A_{\text{samp}} \cdot \delta_{\text{samp}} - \bar{A}_{\text{blk}} \cdot \bar{\delta}_{\text{blk}}}{A_{\text{samp}} - \bar{A}_{\text{blk}}}, \quad \delta_{\text{lin-corr}} = \delta_{\text{blk-corr}} - k_{\text{area}} \cdot \left(A_{\text{samp}} - \bar{A}_{\text{anchor}}\right)$$
C. First-Order Gaussian Error Propagation (GUM 5.1.2)

Partial differential expansion for the linear calibration function $y_{\text{cal}} = a \cdot x_{\text{raw}} + b$:

$$\sigma_{\text{cal}} = \sqrt{ \left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial x_{\text{raw}}} \sigma_{\text{raw}}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial a} \text{SE}_a\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial b} \text{SE}_b\right)^2 } = \sqrt{ (a \cdot \sigma_{\text{raw}})^2 + (x_{\text{raw}} \cdot \text{SE}_a)^2 + \text{SE}_b^2 }$$
